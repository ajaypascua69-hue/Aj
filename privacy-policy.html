Information you provide

When you use LEO AI, the messages and conversation context you submit are sent to Puter.js AI services and the selected AI model/provider so that a response can be generated. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

Third-party services

LEO AI uses Puter.js to access AI functionality. Your prompts and related context may be processed by Puter and the selected model/provider under their own terms and privacy practices. Their handling, retention, and use of information are controlled by those providers, not by this app publisher. Review the current provider terms before using the service. LEO AI does not promise that third-party providers retain no data.

AI chat requires an internet connection. The app also offers browser/device speech input and speech output where supported. Speech recognition may be processed by services chosen by your browser or device; consult those providers' privacy information.

Local app data

The current app keeps the active conversation in the app's running page memory. It provides an optional feature to export a conversation as a text file on your device. The export is initiated by you. The app shell may be cached by the browser's service worker for offline loading. Clearing browser or app data may remove locally held information.

How information is used

Information you submit is used to provide AI responses and operate the features you request. The app publisher does not operate a separate account database or analytics service in this app version. Third-party service providers may process information as described in their own policies.

Children

LEO AI is a general-purpose AI assistant and is not designed specifically for children. A parent or guardian should supervise any use by a child and review the applicable service terms.

Security

Use the app only over a secure internet connection where possible. No internet transmission or software system can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid submitting confidential or highly sensitive information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
